**Prosper or Perish 0.10: The Ox Paradox** is out!

For EU5 1.4.5, with or without Across the Pillars. Not save compatible, so please start a new campaign.

**What's new**
- Local food: no food market. Every province lives off its own store, and the months in store drive growth.
- New food buildings: Tavern, Cookshop, Grange, Victualling Yard, bigger Granaries.
- Arable Land: population capacity is the land that feeds people. Improvements win land, farms take it.
- Navigable Rivers: fleets sail the great rivers far inland.
- New map: climates, vegetation, terrain, soils, fertility and redrawn rivers.
- Eight crop farms, Manual Labor as a good, manufacturing tiers and town rights.
- Logistics: overbuilding costs market access, logistics buildings win it back.
- Smarter AI: builds by prices and shortages, takes connected land, fights more.
- A rewritten, illustrated Europedia.
