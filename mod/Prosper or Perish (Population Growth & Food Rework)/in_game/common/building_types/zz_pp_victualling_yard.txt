# The harbour Yard is the merchants' victualling house: it packs a little of the province store and ships in
# grain from the wider market, so it needs far less of the local food than the Grange and makes twice the victuals.
# Per level: 8 food + 2.33 grain (12 food each in the farms' Provisioning) = 36 food for 1.2 victuals, which a
# Tavern turns back into 36 food: food-neutral, where the Grange loses a quarter (24 food for 0.6 victuals).
# 2026-09-30: every input, output and the food modifier scaled x0.4 (was 20 food + 5.83 grain -> 3.0 victuals),
# together with the Tavern and the Grange; the per-level -5 % victuals stays, so a Yard still tops out at the same
# level. Food capacity (was 100) removed: the Granary is the capacity building. Sailors 0.01 -> 0.003.
# slot 0: shipping the province's own food out; the inputs are the cost of the shipment, and the better ships lose
# less of the cargo: River Barges 0.2, Merchantmen 0.268, Armed Convoy 0.32 victuals from the 8 food. The labour share
# falls from method to method (54 %, 12 %, 4 %), so dear labour moves a harbour up the list. The raw_modifier's -5 %
# victuals per level keeps the Yard from making food: a convoy Yard makes (0.32 + 0.932) x 0.95 = 1.19 victuals from
# 36 food at one level, and every further level loses another 5 %.

# No storage leg (2026-09-26): the great harbours live on imported grain and are short of food by nature, so the
# Yard's profit follows only the victuals price against grain and shipping. Per level at base prices (victuals 3,
# grain 1): about +0.7..+0.9 gold at level 1, falling by the -5 % per level to 0 around level 5.

# per Yard level, staffed or not: every level ships a little less (-5 % victuals), so a big Yard never makes food and
# grows with diminishing returns (its free-building-level cost was removed 2026-09-30: supported levels come only from
# location attributes and rank)

	local_population_capacity = 0.12
  local_victuals_output_modifier = -0.05
