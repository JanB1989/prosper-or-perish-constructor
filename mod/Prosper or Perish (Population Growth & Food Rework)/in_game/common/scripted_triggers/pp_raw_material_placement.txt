# Prosper or Perish - where a good may become a location's raw material when a script plants it there (the
# Columbian exchange actions move_nw_good_to_new_location / move_ow_good_to_new_location).
# EU5 1.4 moved these tests into the goods' own location_potential (is_goods_valid_for_location); the mod removes
# those potentials and keeps the rules here, as in 1.3: vanilla 1.3's conditions on the World Builder classes.
# A good without a pp_<good>_placement_location has no rule. Location scope. The actions are generated from vanilla by
# worldbuilder/raw_material_placement.py and test these by name (pp_columbian_exchange_rgo_refresh.txt).
