# Prosper or Perish - local RGO flavor bonuses.
# Each raw material good receives a 20% local output baseline plus two or three distinct location-scope effects.
# Side effects stay near the low end of vanilla magnitudes: estates +0.10, migration 0.03-0.10, development 0.02-0.04,
# unrest 0.03-0.05, disease resistance 0.02-0.05, named-disease modifiers 0.05-0.15.
