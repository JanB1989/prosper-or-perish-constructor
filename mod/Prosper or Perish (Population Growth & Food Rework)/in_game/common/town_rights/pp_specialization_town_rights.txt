# Prosper or Perish - specialization town rights, one per refined end good (manufacturing tiers 2026-10-06, Jan),
# plus one iron right, Hammer Privileges (pp_iron_rights, 2026-10-15; see its block).
# Charter: no institution, at least 3 levels of the good's line in the town; output +0.15, production efficiency -0.05.
# Rights (upgrade of the charter): unlocked by the Printing Press advance (Printing Press embraced); until then only the
# charter can be granted, afterwards a charter upgrades and new towns take the rights directly. At least
# 8 levels (cloth, leather, pottery, glass, jewelry, books) or 5 (the other lines); output +0.35,
# production efficiency -0.05. One specialization right per town. The AI values only the output line against what the
# town already makes, so it takes a right where that line dominates the town.
# The vanilla bundled specialization rights and charters are switched off below.
# Each rights block stands before its charter: upgrades_to reads only a key that is already loaded (a forward reference
# fails with "Failed to read key reference" and the charter never upgrades).
