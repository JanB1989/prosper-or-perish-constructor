	# Market food (tested 2026-09-24), each month:
	#   FOOD_PROVINCE_LOCAL_RESERVE_RATIO: a province hands its stored food above ratio x its food capacity to the
	#     market. 1000 = never.
	#   FOOD_CAPACITY_FACTOR: scales market food capacity (factor x about the sum of its provinces' food capacity).
	#     0 = markets hold nothing, so no province can buy market food and markets trade none between them.
	#   Pass 1: provinces with a food shortage get their need from the market first. The two defines below never
	#     affect pass 1.
	#   Pass 2: other provinces refill their storage, but only from market stock above
	#     FOOD_PROPORTION_TO_PRESERVE_IN_MARKET_IF_POSSIBLE x market food capacity (vanilla 0.25), and each province
	#     takes at most FOOD_PASS2_MAX_MONTHS_OF_DEFICIT x its monthly deficit (vanilla 3). If that product is 0
	#     (no deficit, or the define is 0) there is no cap.
	#   FOOD_PRICE: an empty market's food price sits exactly at it. The food slider bills
	#     needed food x FOOD_PRICE x slider, even when the market delivers nothing.
	#   Goods with a food value carry market food when traded; from an empty market those trades never run.
	#     All food goods here have food = 0 (goods/pp_goods_adjustments.txt), so they trade as normal goods.
