# Country start adjustments (docs/country_finetunes.md): one effect per country holds its change to the vanilla
# treasury, so the numbers live here only. on_action/pp_country_finetunes.txt applies it to an AI country on the first
# day of a new game; a human player of the country gets its event (events/pp_start_adjustment_events.txt), whose accept
# option calls the same effect and whose decline option keeps the vanilla start. Aims, conquest preferences, opinions
# and advances are no start adjustments: they stay where they are.
