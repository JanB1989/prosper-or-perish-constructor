# Merchant houses (2026-11-24, Jan): where a Trade Office, Fondaco or Hanseatic Kontor may stand (their allow; the
# rank rule sits on the buildings). A market centre, a megalopolis, or a port with a great natural harbour: 0.75 is
# vanilla's "deep bay" step and admits about 170 ports of the 1337 map outside market centres (51 of them towns or
# cities at the start). Owner-independent, but it sits in allow (never location_potential): market centres can move.
# AGENTS.md "Merchant Houses".
pp_merchant_house_site = {
	OR = {
		is_market_center = yes
		location_rank = location_rank:megalopolis
		AND = {
			is_port = yes
			modifier:natural_harbor_suitability >= 0.75
		}
	}
}
