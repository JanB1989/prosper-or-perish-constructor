not dated tree canopy. The earlier placement algorithm conserved this class
into land independently classified by PNV as grassland. It consequently marked
Guenoa as approximately 98% forest. The replacement splits remaining natural
land using the independent potential-vegetation classes, with a documented
uniform allocation assumption within coarse LUH2 cells. PNV is also potential,
not observed medieval cover. Unknown PNV and source-mask gaps remain separate

fraction and coverage remain diagnostic fields. Managed pasture and rangeland
both enter the exclusive grazing account. Baseline open cultivation displaces
known open wild land first, then grazing; it cannot consume unknown land while
